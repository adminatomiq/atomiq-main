Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Atomiq is a study app for Class 12 PCMB students. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile photo.
  • What you enter when registering: full name, phone number and stream (PCM, PCB or PCMB).
  • Your study activity: quiz results (subject, chapter, score, date), chapters you have opened, and leaderboard points.
  • Messages you send through Contact Us.
  • A profile photo, if you choose to upload one.

How we use it

  • To run your account and show your progress and profile.
  • To rank students on the leaderboard. Only your name, stream and points are shown to other students. Your email and phone number are never shown to them.
  • To reply to your messages and improve the app. Admins can see registered student details and results to support students.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Registration and Contact Us messages are also emailed to the Atomiq admin. We do not sell your data or show ads based on it.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from Profile in the app. This removes your profile, photo, progress and leaderboard places. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Contact

Questions? Email admin.atomiq@gmail.com or use Contact Us in the app.
